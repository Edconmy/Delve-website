How I work

Three ways to engage.

Every engagement is different. The model adapts to what you actually need - from a rapid diagnostic sprint to full organisational design and embedded delivery capability.

1 week — In studio

Organisational readiness sprint

A high-energy, condensed engagement designed to rapidly diagnose capability gaps and develop targeted interventions. For organisations that need to act on a focused challenge quickly.

  • Validated capability improvement opportunities
  • Prioritised organisational design concepts
  • Pilot intervention and scoping plan

8 - 12 weeks — In studio

Full organisational design & capability project

A comprehensive engagement from initial capability assessment through to validated interventions and implementation roadmap. Research-led, stakeholder-validated, built for delivery.

  • Stakeholder alignment and buy-in
  • Deep insight through employee and customer research
  • Validated organisational design concepts
  • Pilot intervention tested with users
  • Follow-up implementation plan

13+ weeks — In house

Implementation - embedding capability

Transforming organisational design concepts into embedded capability. I help organisations transition from design to delivery by providing expert support and burst capacity throughout execution.

  • Capability audit and team structure review
  • Specialist sourcing and onboarding
  • Flexible resource - on and off as needed
  • Knowledge transfer to build internal capability
  • One invoice per month
